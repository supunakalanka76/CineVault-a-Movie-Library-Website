Batman Returns

Batman Returns is a 1992 American superhero film directed by Tim Burton, based on the DC Comics character Batman. It is the sequel to Burton's 1989 film Batman and stars Michael Keaton as the title character, alongside Danny DeVito, Michelle Pfeiffer, Christopher Walken, and Michael Gough.

Wild Wild West

Wild Wild West is a 1999 American steampunk Western action-comedy film directed by Barry Sonnenfeld. The film stars Will Smith and Kevin Kline as two U.S. Secret Service agents who are sent to the Old West to investigate the assassination of President Ulysses S. Grant and to stop a diabolical inventor from using his mechanical spider to take over the United States.

Amazing Spiderman

The Amazing Spiderman is a 2012 American superhero film directed by Marc Webb, based on the Marvel Comics character Spider-Man. It stars Andrew Garfield as the title character, alongside Emma Stone, Rhys Ifans, and James Franco.
